# OWASP Top 10 & Master Prompt §11 Security Audit Report

**Project:** NextStopTurkey — Luxury Editorial Travel & Tour Platform (`web-next/` + `backend/`)  
**Audit Date:** 2026-11-07  
**Status:** Verified & Hardened (Phase 9)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

All 24 client-side hooks and components in `web-next/` were audited and migrated away from `localStorage.getItem('authToken')` to the centralized `api` client (`web-next/src/lib/api/client.ts`) with `withCredentials: true`. Session management now uses **`HttpOnly`, `Secure`, `SameSite=Lax` `__Host-nst_access` (15-minute TTL) and `__Host-nst_refresh` (7-day rotating TTL)** cookies issued by the authoritative Express backend, paired with automatic refresh token reuse detection, CSRF `Origin`/`Referer` validation, server-side RBAC (`adminMiddleware`), and per-endpoint rate limiting.
